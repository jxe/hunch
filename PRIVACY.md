# Privacy policy

Hunch stores your notes as plain `.md` files in a folder you choose. The
folder lives on your own device — and, if you point Hunch at a folder
in iCloud Drive, in your own iCloud. Nothing is sent to a Hunch server,
because there is no Hunch server.

## Data collection

None. Hunch does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
information, usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.

## Network usage

When you paste an external URL into a note, Hunch fetches the page's
favicon and title to render the link as a chip. The request goes
directly from your device to the site you linked to, via Apple's
`LinkPresentation` framework. No request goes through any
Hunch-controlled server.

## Voice transcription

If you use the microphone button or the Siri Shortcut, audio is
transcribed on-device by Apple's `SFSpeechRecognizer`. Microphone
access is requested only when you initiate a recording.

## iCloud

If you choose to keep your Hunch folder inside iCloud Drive, your
notes sync via your own iCloud account. Hunch has no access to your
iCloud credentials and reads nothing else from your iCloud.
